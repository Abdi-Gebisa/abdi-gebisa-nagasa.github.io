About Me

I am a Grade 12 student at Ambo University Special Secondary School (AUSSS) in Ethiopia, bridging theoretical & computational physics with software engineering. Coming from a rural background, I leveraged self-education and STEM resources to investigate fundamental symmetries in subatomic physics while engineering software applications for my local community.

Research & Projects

Computational Physics / Python

Time-Reversal Violation in Beta Decay

Formulated an independent experimental & Monte Carlo simulation concept exploring the $T$-odd scalar triple product $C_T = \mathbf{p}_\beta \cdot (\mathbf{p}_\gamma \times \mathbf{p}_{\text{recoil}})$ in radiative nuclear beta decay to isolate fundamental symmetry violation from final-state interaction (FSI) backgrounds.

CERN BL4S / Particle Physics

Served as Theoretical Physics Lead for a CERN Beamline for Schools proposal analyzing relativistic lepton decay spectra and boundary conditions. Reviewed and guided by Raji Ashenafi (MIT PhD Candidate) and Dr. Tolasa (Dean of Physics, Ambo University).
