02 / Research interests

Questions worth
investigating.

Current areas of academic curiosity, informed by independent study, student research proposals, and scientific problem-solving.

R / 01Theoretical physics

T-odd correlations in beta decay

Studying T-odd momentum correlations proposed in radiative beta decay, with attention to electromagnetic final-state interactions and the distinction between a T-odd observable and evidence of time-reversal violation.

CT = p⃗β · (p⃗γ × p⃗recoil)
R / 02Particle physics

Muon decay and measurement

For CERN Beamline for Schools, I contributed as the theoretical-physics team member to a proposal investigating precise boundary measurement of muon decay and related relativistic considerations. The proposal received a certificate recognising its scientific merit; it was not selected for the experiment phase.

Focus: decay kinematics · measurement precision · relativistic physics
R / 03Quantum computing

Quantum information

Interested in the foundations of quantum mechanics, quantum information, and how computational approaches can help investigate problems that are difficult to address with classical methods.

R / 04Scientific computing

Physics through computation

Exploring how code, numerical methods, and interactive visualisation can support scientific learning, modelling, and experimentation.
