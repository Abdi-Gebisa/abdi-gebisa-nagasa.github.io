Research

My current work is centered on precision tests of fundamental symmetries in nuclear beta decay.

Experimental and Computational Search for Time-Reversal Violation in Radiative Nuclear Beta Decay

I am developing a research proposal around a T-odd momentum correlation in radiative nuclear beta decay. The proposed concept considers beta-particle, photon, and recoil detection, together with timing and coincidence information, to reconstruct the relevant correlation and search for a measurable asymmetry.

CT = pβ · (pγ × precoil)     |     AT = [N(CT>0) − N(CT<0)] / [N(CT>0) + N(CT<0)]

A central part of the proposal is distinguishing a genuine fundamental T-violation signal from electromagnetic final-state interactions (FSI) and detector-induced false asymmetries.

Nuclear Physics Fundamental Symmetries Radiative Beta Decay Precision Measurement

Lead Researcher: Abdi Gebisa

Technical Feedback: Dr. John A. Behr (Senior Research Scientist, TRIUMF / TRINAT)
