Discrete Symmetries • Weak Interaction 2024 – Present

Precision Search for Time-Reversal Violation in Radiative Nuclear Beta Decay

An experimental proposal investigating discrete symmetry breaking beyond the Standard Model (BSM). The project designs a coincidence measurement framework targeting the T-odd scalar triple product among the beta particle, emitted photon, and daughter recoil nucleus:

CT = pβ ⋅ (pγ × precoil)

Under time reversal (T), linear momentum vectors reverse sign (p⃗ → -p⃗), causing CT → -CT. A statistically non-vanishing asymmetry signals a T-odd interaction:

AT =
N(CT > 0) − N(CT < 0) N(CT > 0) + N(CT < 0)

The research focuses on distinguishing genuine fundamental T-violation from false asymmetries induced by electromagnetic final-state interactions (FSI).

Lead Researcher: Abdi Gebisa Technical Feedback: Dr. John A. Behr (Senior Research Scientist, TRIUMF / TRINAT) Target Facilities: CERN-ISOLDE, TRIUMF/TRINAT, FRIB
CERN Recognized • Relativistic Kinematics 2023 – 2024

Precision Boundary of Relativistic Time Dilation in Low-Momentum Muon Decay

Served as Theoretical Physics Lead for an experimental proposal submitted to the CERN Beamline for Schools (BL4S) competition. Investigated the precision limits where Einstein's relativistic time dilation equation approaches the instrumental resolution floor of particle detectors at lower momentum states (1.0 – 2.5 GeV/c).

τ = γτ0 =
τ0 √(1 − v2 / c2)

Constructed a Python Monte Carlo simulation (N = 10,000 decay events per momentum state) to analyze exponential decay distributions and map statistical uncertainties against detector resolution thresholds.

Credential: Awarded Official CERN BL4S Certificate Advisors: Dr. Tolasa (Ambo University), Raji Ashenafi (MIT) Core Toolkit: Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scintillator Geometries
