Research

My current scientific interests focus on precision tests of fundamental symmetries and computational physics.

Experimental and Computational Search for Time-Reversal Violation in Radiative Nuclear Beta Decay

A proposed precision-physics study of a T-odd momentum correlation in radiative nuclear beta decay. The concept involves beta-particle, photon, and recoil detection, timing/coincidence information, reconstruction of a T-odd observable, and analysis of detector and final-state-interaction effects.

CT = pβ · (pγ × precoil)

A central methodological issue is distinguishing a possible time-reversal-odd signal from electromagnetic final-state interactions (FSI), detector asymmetries, backgrounds, and reconstruction biases.

Nuclear PhysicsFundamental Symmetries Radiative Beta DecayPrecision Measurement

Lead Researcher: Abdi Gebisa Nagasa

Technical Feedback: Dr. John A. Behr — Senior Research Scientist, TRIUMF / TRINAT

Muon Lifetime Measurement — CERN Beamline for Schools

I served as the theoretical lead for a high-school team whose proposal on muon lifetime measurements was scientifically accepted in CERN's Beamline for Schools competition. The team was not selected to travel to CERN for the experimental stage.

Proposal direction: Precision boundary of relativistic time dilation in low-momentum muon decay measurements.
